# AMC v1.8.0 — Autonomy and one-tap local AI

Background tasks now keep going on their own: when a step needs your approval they pause, ask you, and continue after you decide, instead of skipping it. Local AI is set up with one tap and keeps downloading when you leave the app.

## Autonomy

- **Pause, ask, continue:** a scheduled or background task that hits a step needing approval parks it, notifies you (and Telegram, if paired), and resumes exactly that step after **Approve**. The approval is bound to the exact command and arguments; anything else still asks again.
- **Pending approvals** are listed in **Settings → Autonomy** with Approve/Reject, plus a timeout setting.
- **Long runs:** runs save checkpoints and continue after the app was killed or the phone restarted. Long tasks continue in follow-up runs (up to six in a chain).
- **Provider hiccups:** rate limits and server errors are retried with backoff (respecting Retry-After), then the fallback provider takes over. If all fail, the task tries again later.
- **Bridge self-heal:** if the Termux bridge drops during a run, AMC restarts it and continues.
- **Loop detection:** when the agent repeats the same step without progress it gets a hint, and stops after eight repeats.
- **Telegram delivery:** results of background tasks can be sent to Telegram (**Settings → Channels**).
- **Setup:** two new checklist steps, "Let AMC run in the background" (battery) and, on Android 12 and later, how to stop Android from killing Termux processes.

## Local AI in one tap

- **Set up local AI** picks the right model for your phone, downloads it in Termux, checks it, starts llama-server, tests the speed and makes it the active model. Your cloud model stays as fallback.
- **Progress bar** with downloaded size, speed and time left, in the app and in a notification with **Cancel**.
- **Keeps going:** leaving the screen or closing the app no longer stops the setup. Interrupted downloads resume where they stopped, also after a restart or network loss.
- **One fix per error:** no storage, old bridge, network, damaged file and others each show one button that fixes it ("Free up storage", "Update bridge", "Resume", ...).
- **Verified models:** sizes and SHA-256 checksums of all catalog models were checked against Hugging Face; a damaged download is detected and deleted. The Gemma entry was corrected (4.2 GB, phones with 12 GB RAM, experimental).

## Update the bridge

The Termux bridge has new commands for background downloads. Run the installer once more, or tap **Update bridge** when the app asks.

## Under the hood

- Room database version 2 with a tested migration (pending approvals and run checkpoints).
- New unit tests for the approval queue, provider retry, migration and local setup; 340 Android and 51 bridge tests pass.
- CI checks the model catalog against Hugging Face on every change.

## Not checked on a device

This release was built and unit-tested, but parking and resuming approvals, the local AI setup with real downloads, and the Telegram buttons were not exercised on a real phone. Please report anything that does not work as described.
