# AMC v1.6.0 — Interactive

Everything on screen now responds to what you do: press and hold, swipe, tap an example, or try a command before you trust it. Nothing acts on its own. Every action still starts with a tap from you.

## Chat

- **Press and hold a message** to copy it, or to send your own message again.
- **Quick actions and examples fill the input** instead of sending right away. You read the text, change it if you like, and send it.
- **Empty chat:** four example requests. Tap one to edit it.
- **Tool activity is live:** while a command runs, the line shows how long it has been running. Press and hold the line to copy the command. The output is selectable text.
- **Approval turns into the result** with a short crossfade, instead of a hard swap.
- **Plan progress:** a bar fills as steps finish, steps change their icon smoothly, and the summary says "Plan complete" at the end.
- **Haptic feedback** when you send, approve or reject, stop, or press and hold.

## Chats drawer

- **Swipe a chat to the left** to delete it. The bin icon does the same.
- **Undo** appears for a few seconds. The chat is removed for good only after that, or when you close the drawer.

## Setup

- **A live checklist** replaces the one-off Quick connect card. It shows five steps: Termux, Termux:API, permission to run commands, background running, and the bridge with pairing.
- **One action per step.** The open step is expanded and offers exactly one button, such as "Open F-Droid", "Allow" or "Start & pair".
- **The checklist re-checks** when you come back to AMC and whenever the connection changes.
- **Pairing explains Termux's answer.** A refusal about external apps, a missing bridge, or a missing token each get their own message, and the command to fix it has a copy button.
- **All set:** when every step is done, "Open chat" takes you straight to the chat.

## Settings

- **Try a command:** type a command or tap an example, and see how each security mode would treat it: runs, asks first, or blocked. It uses the same rules the agent uses, and nothing runs. The reason is shown underneath.

## Under the hood

- The Termux pairing answer is classified in one pure function, `TermuxPairing.classify`, and the setup steps in `setupItems` and `nextSetupStep`. Both are unit-tested.
- `PolicySimulator` reuses `CommandSecurityFilter`, so the simulator and the agent cannot disagree.
- New unit tests: 23 (151 in total).

## Not checked on a device

Swiping, the press-and-hold menu and haptics were not exercised on a phone in this release. The visuals were rendered from the real components. Please report anything that feels off.
